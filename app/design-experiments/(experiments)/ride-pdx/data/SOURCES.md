# Ride PDX — data provenance

`places.json` and `bikes.json` are generated by `_build.py` (re-run: `python3 _build.py`).
Snapshot taken 2026-10-22T06:26Z (2026-10-21 evening, Pacific).

The bikes are **live**: the page and `/api/ride-pdx` read the BIKETOWN feed
through `live.ts` every 60 seconds. `bikes.json` is only the fallback, and the
legend says "Snapshot" whenever it is the thing on screen.

## Tier key

- **A** — primary. The agency that owns the data, publishing it directly.
- **B** — aggregator republishing government data.
- **C** — community or crowdsourced.
- **D** — could not fetch, could not verify, or provenance unclear.

## Sources

### BIKETOWN GBFS — Tier A

- **Endpoint:** `https://gbfs.biketownpdx.com/gbfs/gbfs.json` (redirects its feeds to `gbfs.lyft.com/gbfs/{1.1,2.3}/pdx/`)
- **Publisher:** Lyft, which operates BIKETOWN for the Portland Bureau of Transportation
- **Cadence:** live, `ttl: 60`
- **API key:** none. CORS is open, but we proxy anyway so one fetch per minute serves everyone.
- **Verified:** 2026-10-21, returned **308** stations (all installed, 306 renting) and **1,531** free-floating vehicles (1,174 scooters, 357 e-bikes)
- **Fields used:**
  - 1.1 `station_information`: `station_id`, `name`, `lat`, `lon`, `capacity`
  - 1.1 `station_status`: `num_bikes_available`, `num_ebikes_available`, `num_scooters_available`, `num_docks_available`, `is_installed`, `is_renting`
  - 2.3 `free_bike_status`: `lat`, `lon`, `vehicle_type_id` (2 = e-bike, 3 = scooter, per `vehicle_types`), `current_range_meters`, `is_disabled`, `is_reserved`
  - `last_updated` from `free_bike_status` is the "as of" time on the page
- **Coordinate system:** WGS84
- **License:** GBFS public feed, published under Portland's permit requirements

**Why two GBFS versions:** 1.1 splits station counts by e-bike and scooter; 2.3
adds vehicle type and battery range to free vehicles. Each is used for what only
it has.

**What the source does not have:** no vehicle identity that persists between
pulls (bike IDs rotate by design, for rider privacy), so the map cannot show
movement over time. No pricing per trip on the page; that lives in the BIKETOWN app.

**A stale-looking field we do not use:** `last_reported` on 301 of 308 stations
was more than 30 days old. These are "lightweight" stations with no kiosk
hardware, so the timestamp tracks a device that does not exist. Counts at those
stations still change minute to minute, so we show them, and use the feed-level
`last_updated` as the as-of time instead.

**Transformations:** disabled and reserved vehicles dropped; range converted to
miles; coordinates rounded to 5 decimals (about 1 m).

### Portland Parks & Recreation parks — Tier A

- **Endpoint:** `https://www.portlandmaps.com/arcgis/rest/services/Public/Parks_Misc/MapServer/2`
- **Publisher:** City of Portland, PP&R
- **Cadence:** static snapshot
- **Verified:** 2026-10-21, 318 parks, **305** with a pin inside the places extent
- **Fields used:** `OBJECTID`, `NAME`, `ACRES`
- **Transformations:** each park is reduced to one pin at the area-weighted
  centroid of its largest parcel. A long, thin park can have that point fall
  just outside its own boundary. Fine for "ride to this park", not for
  surveying it.

### OpenStreetMap via Overpass — Tier C

- **Endpoint:** `https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter`
- **Cadence:** static snapshot
- **License:** ODbL, credited in the map attribution
- **Verified:** 2026-10-21, inside `45.44,-122.79,45.62,-122.50`:
  - `shop=supermarket|greengrocer`: **117** named (unnamed dropped)
  - `amenity=cafe`: **541** named (4 unnamed dropped)
  - `amenity=library`: **18** public libraries, of 33 tagged
  - `tourism=viewpoint`: **38**, of which 29 have no name and are labeled "Viewpoint"
- **Fields used:** `name`, `brand`, `opening_hours`, `addr:housenumber` + `addr:street`
- **Library filter:** kept Multnomah County Library, Washington County
  Cooperative Library Services, Garden Home Community Library Association, and
  City of Milwaukie, plus two MCL branches (St. Johns, Belmont) that have no
  `operator` tag. Dropped university and college libraries, a bookshop, a
  private campus library, and the Oregon Historical Society research library.
- **Hours** are shown exactly as OSM writes them (`Mo-Fr 07:00-18:00`). We do
  not parse them into "open now", because a wrong "open" is worse than a raw string.

### OSRM routing (FOSSGIS) — Tier C

- **Endpoint:** `https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-{foot,bike}/{route,table}/v1/driving/...`
- **What it does:** walk and bike times over the OSM street network. Called on
  a tap only (one table call per new start point, two route calls per selected
  place), never on pan or zoom. It is a shared community server.
- **Verified:** 2026-10-21. PSU to NE 7th & Holladay: 40 min walking, 11 min by bike.
- **Fallback:** if it does not answer, times become straight-line distance × 1.3
  at 78 m/min walking and 230 m/min riding, and the page prefixes them with `~`
  and says so under the list.
- **Caveat on bike times:** the bike profile assumes a typical rider. BIKETOWN
  bikes are all electric-assist, so real trips usually run a little faster.

## Real vs illustrative

Every record drawn is real. 308 stations and about 1,500 vehicles from the live
feed, 305 parks from PP&R, 714 places from OSM. Nothing is synthesized. The only
estimated numbers are fallback trip times, which carry a `~` where they appear.

## Could not verify

- **Lime Portland:** the GBFS URL in the MobilityData catalog
  (`data.lime.bike/api/partners/v2/gbfs/portland/gbfs.json`) returns 404, and so
  do the v1 paths. Dropped.
- **Bird Portland:** `mds.bird.co/gbfs/v2/public/portland/` responds, but
  `free_bike_status` held **0** vehicles. Dropped. If Bird returns to Portland,
  it would slot in as a third vehicle source in `live.ts`.

## API keys

None. Every source is keyless.
